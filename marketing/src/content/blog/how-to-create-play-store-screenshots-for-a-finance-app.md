## Trust signals

Security, biometrics, institutions supported, and plain-language insights—**without** fabricated numbers.

## Gallery arc

1. Home dashboard with clarity headline.  
2. Transfers or payments—mask account digits.  
3. Insights / budgeting charts with simple legends.  
4. Cards / limits / controls if differentiators.  
5. Support and statements if relevant.
