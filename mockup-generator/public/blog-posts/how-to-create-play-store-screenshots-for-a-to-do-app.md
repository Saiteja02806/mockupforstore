## What buyers look for

Speed of capture, reliability of reminders, natural language or voice entry, widgets, and sharing without chaos.

## Suggested five-shot arc

1. **Today list** — “Capture tasks in one line.”  
2. **Natural language / quick add** — show the flow honestly.  
3. **Reminders / focus** — respect for notification volume.  
4. **Shared lists** — if you ship collaboration.  
5. **Widgets or wear** — only if real.
