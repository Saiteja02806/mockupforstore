## Why teams avoid Figma for screenshots

Figma shines for product UI design, but **listing composites** repeat the same chores: device bezels, safe margins, export slices, and localization variants. Small teams often want a **shorter path** from emulator PNG to Play-ready JPEG.

## What you still need without Figma

1. **Sharp captures** at real resolutions.  
2. **A compositing surface** for frame + headline + background.  
3. **Export presets** per store track.  
4. **A review step** for thumbnail legibility and policy honesty.  

## Mockup Studio workflow

Most tools stop at placing a capture inside a device frame. Mockup Studio is a **Play Store screenshot editor** workflow: you design the **full** listing image—frame, background, headline text, layout, and export size. Start from the [Google Play screenshot editor](/play-store-screenshot-editor/) (same tool, Android-first listing path) or the [app screenshot mockup editor](/app-screenshot-mockup-editor/) mindset: one canvas for the **entire** image. Pair with [how to create Play Store screenshots](/blog/how-to-create-play-store-screenshots/) and the [size guide](/blog/play-store-screenshot-size-guide/).

## When Figma still wins

Highly custom illustration-heavy listings, or brand systems that mandate specific vector components—bring Figma for **hero** art, then export UI captures for compositing if needed.
