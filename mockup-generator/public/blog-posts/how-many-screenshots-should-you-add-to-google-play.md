## The short answer

Most successful consumer apps ship **four to eight** strong phone screenshots. Add more only when each frame introduces **new information**—not variants of the same screen with different headlines.

## Why count matters

Every slot competes for attention in the horizontal gallery. Weak or duplicate slides dilute the story and train users to swipe past your art. Google Play allows multiple images per form factor; the constraint is **quality**, not filling every slot.

## Phone vs tablet

- **Phone:** Lead with your best arc—onboarding promise, core loop, differentiator, trust.  
- **Tablet:** If you support large screens, add **distinct** compositions; do not stretch phone masters.  

See [Play Store screenshot size guide](/blog/play-store-screenshot-size-guide/) and [Play Store vs App Store screenshots](/blog/play-store-vs-app-store-screenshots/).

## When fewer is better

Early-stage apps with narrow feature sets often convert better with **tight** sets than with ten repetitive captures.

## When more helps

Complex categories (creative tools, finance, games with many modes) may justify **eight+** if each slide shows a **mode** users would search for.
