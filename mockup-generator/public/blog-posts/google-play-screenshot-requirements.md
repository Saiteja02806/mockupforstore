## Official source of truth

Bookmark **Google Play Console Help** for preview assets and screenshot requirements. Numbers and rules change; your release checklist should always point to **current** help articles, not static blog tables alone.

## What listings typically need

- **Phone screenshots** for default consumer discovery.  
- **Tablet / large-screen** assets when those tracks are enabled and truthful.  
- **Feature graphic** (where applicable) is **not** interchangeable with arbitrary phone PNGs—export per spec.  

## Honesty and clarity

Google discourages **misleading** preview art and unreadable promotional density. Pair policy reading with [common Play Store screenshot mistakes](/blog/common-play-store-screenshot-mistakes/).
