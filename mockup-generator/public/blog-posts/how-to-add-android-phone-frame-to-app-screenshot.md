## Why frames matter

Frames anchor the eye and communicate **real hardware context**. They also hide awkward aspect mismatches between emulator captures and store-required ratios—when used honestly.

## Pick a frame family

- **Punch-hole / dynamic island class:** reads “current flagship.”  
- **Notch:** still common; good for broad device sympathy.  
- **Borderless / clean:** minimal chrome for brand-forward shots.  
- **Foldable:** only if you ship fold support—otherwise skip.  

Browse shells on [/frames/](/frames/) and open the [Android app screenshot editor](/android-app-screenshot-editor/). When you need to tune **background, headline text, spacing, and export**—not only the bezel—use the [Play Store screenshot editor](/play-store-screenshot-editor/) so you edit the full Google Play mockup.

## Safe areas and headlines

Keep **status bar** clutter out of marketing shots where possible. Place headlines in a **consistent band** (top or bottom third) so the gallery feels designed, not pasted.

## Export with Play in mind

Match **short edge** and **aspect** requirements for the phone track—see [Play Store screenshot size guide](/blog/play-store-screenshot-size-guide/).
