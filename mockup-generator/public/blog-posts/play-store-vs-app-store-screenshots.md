## Strategic difference

**Play** often surfaces screenshots in **search/browse** contexts with aggressive downscaling. **App Store** presentation differs by device class and locale—yet both reward **thumbnail legibility** and honest UI.

## Size and track differences (high level)

- Treat **phone, tablet, and optional other classes** as separate masters—not one stretched export.  
- Apple documents JPEG/PNG requirements and counts in App Store Connect materials—see Apple’s screenshot specifications and our [App Store screenshot requirements](/blog/app-store-screenshot-requirements-complete-guide/).  
- Google publishes Play preview asset rules—pair with [Google Play screenshot requirements](/blog/google-play-screenshot-requirements/) and our [Play size guide](/blog/play-store-screenshot-size-guide/).  

## Reuse strategy

Reuse **underlying UI captures**, not final flattened composites—recompose headlines and padding per aspect.
