## Stand out with specificity

Generic “AI magic” slides convert weakly. Show **real** prompts, grounded answers, privacy toggles, and offline/latency advantages if true.

## Shot suggestions

1. Chat with headline on **trust or accuracy**.  
2. Output type (doc, code, image) tied to a persona.  
3. Settings: model choice, data controls, enterprise features.  
4. Pricing transparency if freemium.
