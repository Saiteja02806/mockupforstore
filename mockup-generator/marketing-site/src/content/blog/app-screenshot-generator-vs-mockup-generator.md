## Definitions

- **Generator:** emphasizes **speed** from templates or presets—great for first drafts.  
- **Mockup generator/editor:** emphasizes **control** over the composite—frame, background, text, spacing, export.  

## When generators shine

Early validation, hackathon builds, or internal pitches where **perfect brand polish** is secondary.

## When mockup editors shine

Public store listings, localized sets, and teams that need **repeatable systems** without redrawing bezels per slide.
