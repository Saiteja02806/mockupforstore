## Goals for store typography

1. **Readable** at thumbnail scale.  
2. **Consistent** across the gallery.  
3. **On-brand** with your product (not a random display face per slide).  
4. **Legally safe**—license webfonts if you embed rasterized type in marketing PNGs.  

## Practical pairings

- **Sans + sans:** one family, two weights (700 headline / 500 subhead).  
- **Humanist sans** for friendly consumer apps; **geometric** for dev tools—match audience expectations.  
- Avoid **ultra-condensed** faces for body-sized screenshot text—they collapse in JPEG.

## Size and export

Raster exports mean type is **pixels**. Test after export, not only in vector tools. See [how to add text](/blog/how-to-add-text-to-app-screenshots/).

## Background contrast

Thin weights on busy gradients fail. Either **darken** the band behind type or **stroke** sparingly—prefer layout fixes over gimmicks.
