## Why Photoshop is overkill for many listings

Photoshop is powerful for retouching and complex composites, but **store screenshots** are often repetitive: same frame, same headline band, same export dimensions. Teams want speed without losing **pixel truth** in the UI layer.

## What to replicate without PS

- Non-destructive-ish **placement** of captures inside frames  
- **Type** layers with consistent styles  
- **Backgrounds** that separate from UI  
- **Export** at exact sizes per store
