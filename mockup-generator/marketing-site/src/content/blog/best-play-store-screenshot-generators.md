## What “generator” should mean for Play listings

A useful generator gets you from **raw captures** to **store-ready composites** quickly: device frame, background, headline type, and export at the right resolution. Tools that only drop a PNG into a static template stall the moment you need brand-specific layout or localization.

## Criteria we use when evaluating tools

1. **Frame fidelity** — believable Android hardware, not clip-art phones.  
2. **Typography control** — weights, spacing, and safe margins for thumbnails.  
3. **Export discipline** — per-track dimensions, not one-size “export PNG.”  
4. **Iteration speed** — tweak copy without rebuilding the whole file from scratch.  
5. **Honesty** — no fake device ratios or misleading “AI #1 app” badges.  

## Mockup Studio (this product)

**Strengths:** Full mockup editing in the browser—frame, background, text, layout, export—with real device frames synced to the editor. **Trade-offs:** You bring honest UI captures; it is not a magic “generate my UI” tool.

## Other categories of tools (compare fairly)

- **Design suites (Figma, etc.):** Maximum flexibility; higher manual labor for device chrome and repeated exports. See [create app screenshots without Figma](/blog/create-app-screenshots-without-figma/) if you want to avoid that tax.  
- **Batch templating apps:** Fast until you need per-slide exceptions.  
- **Pure capture tools:** Great for truth in pixels; weak for listing storytelling unless paired with a compositor.  

We do not publish unverifiable rankings of competitors—requirements differ by team size and brand guidelines.
