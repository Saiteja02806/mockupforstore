## Buyer motivations

Progress visibility, coaching clarity, wearable support, and social accountability—**without** overpromising outcomes.

## Gallery ideas

1. Live workout or heart-rate UI.  
2. History / trends with legible charts.  
3. Programs or levels.  
4. Community challenges (policy-safe).  
5. Integrations (Apple Health / Google Fit) if disclosed accurately.  

## Compliance note

Avoid sensational **before/after** body claims unless substantiated and allowed for your category.
