## Principles

- **Contrast first:** the phone and text must pop.  
- **One system per listing:** do not switch from neon to paper mid-gallery without intent.  
- **Thumbnail test:** blur your eyes—do headline and UI still separate?  

## Patterns that work

1. **Soft vertical gradients** — depth without noise.  
2. **Brand flat + subtle noise** — modern, minimal.  
3. **Radial glow** behind device—focus attention.  
4. **Split-tone** bands for headline vs UI zones.  
5. **Paper / fabric texture** at low opacity—warmth without clutter.  

## Patterns to avoid

- Stock photos that **compete** with UI.  
- Ultra-busy patterns behind small type.  
- **Pure white** behind light silver phones without separation.
